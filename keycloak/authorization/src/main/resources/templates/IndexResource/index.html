Keycloak Authorization

Policy Decision Point Demo

This demo showcases Keycloak as an Authorization PDP with two protected endpoints:

  • /users/{username} — Accessible if it's your own profile or you have the manager role
  • /teams/{teamname} — Requires manager role and matching team attribute

Log in with Keycloak SSO to get started. You will be redirected to your user profile.
